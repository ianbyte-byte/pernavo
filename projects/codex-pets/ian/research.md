# Hearts2Hearts IAN public-profile research

Research date: 2026-07-25. This material is used to shape a fictionalized, age-appropriate human Codex Pet. Interview statements are personality evidence, not agency-authored character canon.

## Verified public facts

- IAN is one of the eight members of Hearts2Hearts.
- SM Entertainment debuted Hearts2Hearts on 2025-02-24 with `The Chase`.
- Seventeen-Web records IAN's birthday as 2009-10-09.
- The reviewed sources consistently use the stage name `IAN` / `이안`.

## Interview-backed character cues

- She describes herself as emotionally perceptive, people-loving, positive, hardworking for the stage, fan-minded, candid, and someone who likes making people laugh.
- She presents a useful contrast: bright and energetic with members, sometimes sentimental when alone.
- She calls herself an energy source for the group and emphasizes listening, feedback, mutual support, and staying happy together.
- Multiple interview/profile sources highlight unusually varied and readable stage expressions.
- She has described pre-camera nerves, followed by focus and enjoyment once performing.
- She thinks carefully about how to present her parts so the public will enjoy them.
- Everyday cues include comparing prices and shipping with image search, convenience-store ice cream, tonkotsu ramen, and being good at cooking ramen.

## Fact boundaries

- `정이안` and the Chinese rendering `郑以安` are widely circulated, but this research did not find a first-party artist profile confirming them as legal-name forms.
- No reviewed first-party source confirmed an official member color, personal emoji, MBTI, or fixed formal position.
- Ray's description of IAN as a center and mood maker is magazine wording, not a formal SM position announcement.
- Navy, black-and-white, and lemon-yellow are palettes from the supplied photos, not official member colors.
- A member's cat comparison may inform sparkling eyes and playful timing only. The Codex Pets remain fully human with no animal anatomy.

## Design handoff

Use the shared personality arc `trace of pre-camera nerves -> focused enjoyment -> confident warmth`. Keep the lower body registered, let eyes and eyelids lead gaze, then use restrained head/neck motion and natural human-hair follow-through. Preserve age-appropriate, modest, nonsexualized styling.
