# Look mechanics — Ian Lemon Cadet

The feet, lower torso, sailor skirt hem, boots, bow placement, and practical body scale stay anchored.  Ian's eyes lead each gaze with coordinated eyelid and eyebrow adjustment; her head and neck follow with a restrained turn or pitch, and the upper torso follows subtly without whole-sprite rotation.  Twin ponytails follow the head turn slightly while remaining ordinary human hair.  The worn navy rosette and silver-heart stay attached to the bodice and turn with it.

- `000` up: eyes and chin lift, upper eyelids open slightly, neck lengthens.
- `090` screen-right: nose tip and pupils turn toward the viewer's right; the viewer-left cheek becomes more visible and the near ponytail/body edge follows.
- `180` down: eyes and chin lower, upper body has a small forward attentive pitch.
- `270` screen-left: nose tip and pupils turn toward the viewer's left; the viewer-right cheek becomes more visible and the opposite ponytail/body edge follows.

Every 22.5-degree pose is an even interpolation between adjacent cardinal families.  No new eyes, pupil-only sliding, whole-body rotation, skew, scale change, or prop replacement is permitted.
