# Ian look mechanics

Ian keeps her boots and lower torso anchored on one baseline. Her long oval face and upper body lead the gaze; the head turns naturally with the eyes and eyelids, while copper-brown hair follows with only a small delayed sway. The sailor cap, bow, collar, and pinafore remain stable with no prop substitution.

- `000 up`: face broadly frontal, eyes and chin lift slightly.
- `090 screen-right`: nose tip and facial plane turn toward viewer-right; the viewer-left face edge recedes.
- `180 down`: eyes lower, eyelids narrow slightly, chin dips without hiding the face.
- `270 screen-left`: inverse of 090; nose tip and facial plane turn viewer-left.

Each 22.5-degree step uses an even, small change of eye aim, head turn, and upper-body counterbalance. No whole-sprite rotation, rescaling, or lateral registration shift is allowed.
